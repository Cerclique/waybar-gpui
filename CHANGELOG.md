## [0.1.0](https://github.com/Cerclique/waybar-gpui/releases/tag/v0.1.0) - 2026-10-25

The first release: write a GPUI panel in Rust, click a Waybar bar module on
Hyprland (0.56) and it opens as a popover next to the bar. Built on
`gpui-pre =0.3.9`; on crates.io as `waybar_gpui`.

### Added

- **SDK** (`waybar_gpui`): the `Panel` trait (a name and a constructor taking
  the panel arguments; built fresh each time its popover opens), `App` (program
  ID, `theme`, `panel`, `run`), `card()` and `Theme` (Catppuccin Mocha by
  default), `close()`, and GPUI re-exported as `waybar_gpui::gpui`.
- **`components` feature**: gpui-component 0.8.0 widgets (as
  `waybar_gpui::components`) with their icons, themed from `Theme`.
- **Command line** of every program: `toggle`, `close`, `quit`, `daemon`,
  `preview`, and a usage text printing a ready-to-paste `on-click` line per
  panel.
- **Runtime**: a daemon per program, spawned by the first click and kept
  between opens. The popover opens on the monitor under the cursor, next to the
  clicked bar module, on any bar edge, and is dismissed by Esc or a click
  outside the card. At most one popover is open across every program. A rebuilt
  or reinstalled program replaces its running daemon on the next click. The
  daemon logs to `$XDG_STATE_HOME/waybar-gpui/<id>.log`.
- **Example panels** (`example-panels`): workspace, clock and, with
  `components`, controls; with `make dev`, a second Waybar (top and left bars)
  to try them on.
- **Starter template**: `cargo generate … template --tag v0.1.0`, with or
  without `components`.
- **Documentation**: README quickstart, the author's guide (writing panels to
  installing, troubleshooting and limits), a page for people who install a
  program, full rustdoc, architecture notes and ADRs.
